The limit shouldn't
be a surprise.

Most AI apps make you guess. You chat, you work, you get in the flow — then suddenly you're rate-limited. Zero Grok gives you a visual warning before the wall appears.

01

Invisible

Your remaining usage is often buried behind settings, rolling windows, or not exposed at all.

02

Limits arrive at the worst possible time: halfway through a task, call, or late-night rabbit hole.

03

FEATURES

Built to stay out of
your way.

Small, fast, local. No account. No cloud. Just a can that tells the truth about your limits.

Always-on meter

A floating can that shows remaining usage at a glance. No need to open a menu or dig through settings.

Live refill timers

See estimated refill times when a provider exposes a reset window — plan around the limit instead of discovering it mid-task.

Four providers, one view

Grok, Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini side by side. Switch focus without opening four different dashboards.

Local-only privacy

Usage data stays on your machine. Nothing is sent to a Zero Grok server because there isn't one.

Drag & save position

Put the can where it helps you most. Position is remembered per provider.

Limit alerts

Optional thresholds so you know when you're getting close — without noise.
