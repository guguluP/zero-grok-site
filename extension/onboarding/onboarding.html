Welcome to Zero Grok

Watch your AI usage drain like a soda can. Never get rate-limited by surprise.

What it does

  • Live remaining % on a floating soda can, with a reset countdown
  • Grok, Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini built in — Perplexity, DeepSeek, Le Chat, Copilot and Meta AI optional
  • Every number is labelled: site usage API, read from page, or estimate
  • Alerts at your thresholds, quiet hours, and a hint when another AI has room
  • Everything stays in your browser — no servers, no analytics

1 · Pick your AIs and plans

Your plan sets sensible defaults (for example, rough caps used only when a site shows no numbers). Optional sites will ask for access.

2 · Look & feel

Quick tips

Open a supported site while signed in and the can appears. Click it for details, drag it to any corner, or press Alt+Shift+Z. For Gemini, open gemini.google.com/usage once.
