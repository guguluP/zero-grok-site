Welcome to Zero Grok

Watch your AI usage drain like a Diet Coke can.
Never get rate-limited by surprise.

What it does

  • Live remaining % on a floating soda can
  • Grok — free rolling limits + SuperGrok weekly pool
  • Claude — session & weekly usage (when available)
  • ChatGPT — Plus/Team live % · Free best-effort
  • Gemini — Free / Plus / Pro / Ultra from usage page
  • Click the can for details · badge shows lowest remaining %
  • Everything stays in your browser — no servers, no tracking

How it works

  1. Open grok.com, claude.ai, chatgpt.com, or gemini.google.com while signed in.
  2. The can appears automatically (bottom-right by default).
  3. For free Grok: send one short message if numbers don’t show yet.
  4. Claude, ChatGPT & Gemini use the same session cookies the site already has.
  5. Click the can anytime for remaining count, reset hint, and refresh.

Quick tip

Free Grok uses the rate-limits API (no Settings page). Claude Pro/Max and Gemini usage pages are the most accurate. ChatGPT Free is best-effort until a limit banner appears. The can-pop plays on first show and again when a window refills after 100% used.

You can change these anytime from the extension Options page.
